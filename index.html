Successive Division by 2 with Remainders

How it works: We divide the ASCII decimal number successively by 2. Each step yields a quotient and a remainder ($0$ or $1$).

Reading Order: Read remainders from BOTTOM to TOP to form the raw binary sequence. Then, apply 8-bit byte padding if enabled!
